Placement Attributes
• Constitutes third year of four-year sandwich degree
• Available to all full-time computing students
• Must be a minimum of 40 full-time weeks
• Of a suitable level of responsibility to add value to your degree
• Must be financially sustainable (Adult 21+ Minimum UK wage)
• All Placements must be approved by the University prior to signing contracts
• International (Tier 4) students can participate in this programme as part of their
degree studies.
