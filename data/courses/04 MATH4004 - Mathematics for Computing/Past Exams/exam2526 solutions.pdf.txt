C(9,8) = 9.

d. Suppose the 8-player squad is required to contain all three goalkeepers, and
the captain and co-captain cannot both be goalkeepers.
Across all squads containing all three goalkeepers, how many valid captain–co-
captain assignments are possible?
2 marks
Number of squads with all three goalkeepers: choose 5 outfield players from 9,
C(9,5) = 126.
From part c, there are 56 possible role assignments per squad.
Invalid assignments are those where the two roles go to goalkeepers:
P(3,2) = 6.
Hence, the number of valid role assignments per squad is
56−6 = 50.
Therefore, the total number of valid assignments across all such squads is
126×50 = 6300.
