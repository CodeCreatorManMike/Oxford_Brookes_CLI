5. a. A committee of 10 people needs to be selected from a group of 5 staff mem-
bers and 11 students.
i. How many different committees of 10 people can be formed, irrespective of
them being staff or students?
2 marks
There are 16 people in total. Since the order does not matter and a single
person cannot be in the committee twice, we have a combination. So, the
number of possible committees is
16!
C(16,10) = = 8008.
6!10!
[2 marks]
Note: No need to argument it’s a combination. Partial credit may be awarded for
showing some understanding.
ii. Upon reflection, it is determined that the committee of 10 people should
contain 3 staff and 7 students. How many ways can such a committee be
selected?
2 marks
We haveC(5,3) = 10 possible staff combinations andC(11,7) = 330
possible student combinations. So, the number of possible committees is
C(5,3)×C(11,7) = 10×330 = 3300.
[2 marks]
b. Elections are being held to choose a president and a vice president for both the
Student Union and the Staff Union. The candidates are 10 staff members and
25 students. How many different ways can the 4 positions (2 people for each
union) be filled?
2 marks
Now the order does matter (president and vice president positions are not inter-
changeable) and a single person cannot be elected twice; hence, we have per-
mutations. We have P(10,2)=90 possible staff election results, and P(25,2)=
600 possible student election results. So, the number of possible election re-
sults is P(10,2)×P(25,2) = 90×600 = 54000.
[2 marks]
