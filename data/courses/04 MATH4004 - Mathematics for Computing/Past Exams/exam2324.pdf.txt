6. Suppose that a computer password is restricted to an alphabet of 50 symbols
and must be exactly 10 symbols long. Suppose there are no further restrictions
to the password.
(a) How many different passwords are there?
2 marks
(b) Suppose that an attacker knows the following information about a
password:
• the attacker knows that the symbols A, B, C, D, E each appear exactly
once;
• the attacker knows that the symbol F appears twice;
• the attacker knows that the symbol G appears three times.
How many possible passwords satisfy the criteria that the attacker knows?
3 marks
Total: 5 marks
