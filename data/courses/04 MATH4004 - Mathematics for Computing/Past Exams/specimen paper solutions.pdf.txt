6. Consider the problem of arranging six objects labelled A, B,C, D, E, and F on
a shelf.
(a) Determine the number of different arrangments of the six objects on the
shelf.
2 marks
In an arrangement the order matters and we are not allowed repetition. So,
we have a permutation. Therefore, there are 6! = 720 different
arrangements.
[2 marks]
(b) How many different arrangements are there if objects A and B are not to be
placed next to each other?
3 marks
The number of arrangements with A and B not next to each other can be
found by subtracting the number of arrangements with A and B next to
each other from the total number of arrangements (720).
In an arrangement with A and B next to each other, we will either have AB
or BA, and in both cases we will have five objects to place. So, there are
5!×2 = 240 arrangements with A and B next to each other.
Hence, there are 720−240 = 480 arrangements with A and B not next to
each other.
[3 marks]
