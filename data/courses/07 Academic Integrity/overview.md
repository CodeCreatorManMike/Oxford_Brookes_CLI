## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course you will be able to:
- Define and describe academic integrity and its connection to your studies.
- Identify and apply good academic practice in your research, reading and writing.
- Recognise and resolve problems with plagiarism and other academic breaches within your own work.

## Course content (TO DO)
1. Understanding academic integrity - define academic integrity and its connection to good academic practice.
2. Beginning an assignment - how to start by engaging your own thinking.
3. Making good reading choices - why use reliable sources and how to find them through the Library.
4. Doing your own work - decisions about academic integrity at each stage of an assignment.
