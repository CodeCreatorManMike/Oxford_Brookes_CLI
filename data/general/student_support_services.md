# Key Student Support Services
Researched: 2026-09-15

## Centre for Academic Development (CAD)
Source: https://www.brookes.ac.uk/students/academic-development
- Free 1:1 tutorials in study skills, maths, and statistics - available from foundation year through PhD level.
- Academic English support: dedicated courses for students studying at a UK university for the first time, plus individual academic-language consultations.
- "Think Space" - a programme of workshops/events to help students make the most of their time at Brookes.
- Online resources: an A-Z of study-skills advice covering referencing, essay writing, revision, exam technique.
- New home: the "Centre for Student Development" at Headington campus (opened 21 September - bookable appointments and drop-in resources).
- Contact: +44 (0)1865 534500 (option 1); academicdev@brookes.ac.uk.

## Library Services
Sources: brookes.ac.uk/library; brookes.ac.uk/library/how-to/reference-and-avoid-plagiarism; brookes.ac.uk/library/collections/librarysearch
- Referencing style: Oxford Brookes primarily uses its own "Brookes Harvard" style (used across most courses university-wide) - NOT a generic "Cite Them Right" house style - however the university licenses and links to the Cite Them Right online platform as a supplementary comprehensive reference guide covering all styles. Other disciplines use different styles: APA (some courses), Chicago (some social sciences, footnote-based), MHRA (English Lit/Creative Writing/Drama/Theatre), OSCOLA (Law). For a Computing/AI student, Brookes Harvard is the expected default unless a specific module states otherwise.
- Reference management: EndNote provided free to all students; Turnitin used for text-matching/plagiarism checks.
- An "Academic Integrity" online course (~1 hour) exists, plus a specific short module addressing how to acknowledge use of AI tools in academic work (see data/courses/07 and 10).
- LibrarySearch / QuickSearch: the library's discovery search tool for books, e-books, journals, databases - 24/7 online access to databases and e-resources via the library website even off-campus.
- Opening hours: Headington library 09:00-22:00 (example day checked was a Tuesday; full weekly schedule at brookes.ac.uk/library/access-and-accessibility/locations-and-opening-hours); Swindon campus library 07:00-19:00.
- Contact: +44 (0)1865 534700 / library@brookes.ac.uk; also live chat support on the library website.
- Study space booking system and accessibility/disability-tailored services are available.

## IT Service Desk
Sources: brookes.ac.uk/it; brookes.ac.uk/study/new-students/student-checklist/get-connected
- Main access point: the ServiceNow Service Portal at https://service.brookes.ac.uk/sp - "one-stop shop" for day-to-day IT needs (raising tickets, knowledge-base articles, WiFi help, Apps Anywhere, printing, Google Essentials/Workspace, VPN/BIG-IP Edge Client).
- Specific phone number/email/opening hours for the IT Service Desk weren't surfaced in public pages - check the ServiceNow portal directly once enrolled (typically also listed on printed campus signage / student ID card back).
- WiFi/eduroam: eduroam is the main WiFi network for staff and students, recommended to set up immediately after enrolment (device-specific setup guides for Windows/Mac/iOS/Android via the IT Service Desk knowledge base). A newer "OBU_Guest WiFi" network exists for guests/transitional use (24-hour re-registration, available in named buildings: Buckley, Clerici, John Henry Brookes, Lloyd, Sinclair, New Headington Hill Building, The Workshop). Legacy "BrookesWifi" network also still referenced. Halls of residence use a separate provider, Wifinity, with its own onboarding network/portal.
- Password resets: not explicitly detailed in public pages - expected to run through the ServiceNow self-service portal; check the ServiceNow KB directly once you have your login.

## Student Wellbeing / Support
Source: https://www.brookes.ac.uk/students/support (and /students/counselling, /students/student-welfare, /students/exams/support)
- Counselling Service - individual therapy sessions, contactable via brookes.ac.uk/students/counselling/contact-us.
- Student Welfare and Support Advice Team - general pastoral/welfare support.
- Togetherall - a 24/7 online peer-support mental health platform available to all students.
- Multifaith Chaplaincy - pastoral/faith-based support.
- Inclusive Support Service - for students with disabilities/long-term conditions: exam/assessment adjustments, non-medical helper support, assistive technology, and help applying for Disabled Students' Allowance (DSA).
- General contact: a "student support contact form" (target response time: 3 working days); direct emails studentsupportservices@brookes.ac.uk and studentcentral@brookes.ac.uk.
- A dedicated "emergency and 24-hour support" page exists for crisis situations - check brookes.ac.uk directly for current crisis-line numbers if urgently needed.

## Timetabling
Source: https://www.brookes.ac.uk/students/timetabling
- Timetabling team schedules lectures/seminars/practicals into rooms and allocates students to seminar/lab groups.
- Primary tool: TimeEdit (timeedit.net/uk_obu) - personal timetables are also pushed to Google Calendar for convenience.
- Support: timetabling@brookes.ac.uk; a Timetable Support knowledge-base article lives on the ServiceNow portal; a Room Directory (Google Sites) and Room Bookings info also exist.
- Several deeper resources (policy doc, room directory) require Brookes login, so aren't independently verifiable from the public page.
