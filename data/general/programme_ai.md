# BSc (Hons) Artificial Intelligence — Programme Structure
Researched: 2026-09-15
Source: https://www.brookes.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate/artificial-intelligence

- Faculty/School: School of Engineering, Computing and Mathematics (ECM), Headington campus.
- UCAS codes: I400 (BSc), I401 (MSci — same course, extended 4th year).
- Duration: BSc 3 years full-time (4 years with a sandwich placement); MSci 4 years FT (5 with placement). Part-time routes exist (up to 8-9 years).
- Programme framing: puts students "at the cutting edge of global AI developments," combining practical and theoretical grounding to solve real-world problems.
- Accreditation: initial accreditation from the British Computer Society (BCS) referenced on the page.

## Entry requirements (2026 entry)
- Standard offer: 112 UCAS tariff points / A-level BBC / IB 27 / BTEC DMM.
- Contextual offer: 88 tariff points / A-level CCD / IB 24 / BTEC MMM.
- GCSE: Maths grade 4/C and English Language grade 4/C minimum.
- No prior computing background required — Year 1 explicitly "gets you up to speed"; existing computing qualifications don't grant exemption from Year 1 content.
- Computing Foundation Year and international pathway/pre-sessional English options exist for those not meeting direct entry criteria.

## Course structure
Module list is indicative (as published on the marketing page), not the formal Programme Specification — cross-check against your own Moodle-published module list for your specific intake, since specs are revised yearly.

### Year 1 — Foundational Computing (compulsory)
- Problem Solving and Programming (30 credits) — see COMP4004
- Computer Science Applications (30 credits) — see COMP4035
- Mathematics for Computing — see MATH4004
- Introduction to Object-Oriented Programming
- Professional Programming Practice
- ~11 contact hours/week + ~1,200 hours/year independent study expected.

### Year 2 — AI Specialisation (compulsory, 8 modules)
- Foundations of Security
- Software Development in C and C++
- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- Applied Software Engineering
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Foundation of Computation
- Advanced Artificial Intelligence
- Enterprise Engineering
- Optional 12-month paid work placement available between Year 2 and Year 3 (placement fee reduced to £1,350).
- Optional study-abroad/exchange typically taken in Year 2.

### Year 3 (final year for BSc; Year 3 of 4 for MSci) — Advanced Topics (compulsory)
- Computing Project (30 credits)
- Advanced Interfaces
- Application and Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
- Cloud Computing and Internet of Things
- Machine Learning
- Machine Vision
- Advanced Machine Learning and Deep Learning

### MSci Year 4 only (compulsory)
- Artificial General Intelligence
- Advanced Artificial Intelligence Project
- Data Visualisations
- Autonomous Intelligent Systems
- Artificial Intelligence Systems Engineering
- Data Science Foundations

## Credits, teaching, assessment
- Credit structure: modules in 15 or 30-credit units; standard UK model (120 credits/level/year; 360 total for BSc Honours, 480 for MSci).
- Teaching methods: lectures, seminars, practical/hands-on labs, "apprentice style learning," face-to-face Mon-Fri typically 9am-6pm.
- Assessment: all modules include continuous assessment; where used, formal written exams contribute 30%, 50%, or 70% of a module's mark (varies by module).
- Facilities: new Headington Hill buildings — VR cave, robotics labs, digital computing labs, material science and composite labs. See practical_info.md.
- Careers: alumni destinations cited include Google, IBM, Sophos, Elsevier; cites IFS (2018) data on ~£10.3k earnings uplift after 5 years for AI/tech graduates.
- Fees (2026/27): Home FT £9,790/yr; Home PT £1,220/module; sandwich placement year £1,905; International FT £18,250/yr.
