# General Practical Info
Researched: 2026-09-15

## Headington Campus / Computing facilities
Sources: brookes.ac.uk/about-brookes/structure-and-governance/faculties-and-schools/ecm/facilities; brookes.ac.uk/estates-development/headington-hill
- Oxford Brookes has fully consolidated engineering/computing/maths activity onto the Headington campus, having formally vacated the old Wheatley campus at the end of October 2024.
- New purpose-built facilities at Headington Hill (opened from Semester 2 of 2024-25 onward) include: digital computing labs, robotics labs, a digital theatre, a VR cave, material science labs, a composite lab, an autodynamics workshop (automotive/engineering), plus social learning spaces, teaching rooms, and café space.
- This is the environment the COMP/MATH modules physically run in.
- Named buildings referenced elsewhere (WiFi page): Buckley, Clerici, John Henry Brookes, Lloyd, Sinclair, New Headington Hill Building, The Workshop - these appear to be the main building names in current use across Headington campus.

## Online platforms
Source: https://www.brookes.ac.uk/study/new-students/student-checklist/online-platforms
- Student Information portal - main administrative hub: annual enrolment, module registration, requesting official documents (attendance letters, council tax certificates), updating personal details, managing tuition fee payments, and submitting Exceptional Circumstances claims via "My Requests."
- TimeEdit - the timetable viewer (see student_support_services.md).
- Student email - Gmail-hosted, format studentnumber@brookes.ac.uk, auto-created on enrolment; university recommends 2-step verification and using Chrome or the Gmail app.
- Moodle - the VLE: register attendance, access course materials/module content, submit assignments, complete mandatory training modules (e.g. academic integrity, AI-use acknowledgment).
- Note: no single unified portal branded "myBrookes" was confirmed as still in active use - current terminology centres on "Student Information," "Moodle," and "TimeEdit" as the three core systems rather than one unified portal brand.
- All platforms require completed online enrolment before becoming accessible.
